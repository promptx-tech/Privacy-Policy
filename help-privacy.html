1Introduction

Welcome to Help (“we,” “our,” or “the App”), developed and operated by PromptX Tech. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information through industry-standard cryptographic and security practices. This Privacy Policy explains in simple and clear terms what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe when you use our real-time messaging and voice calling application.

2Information We Collect

To provide a secure and seamless experience, we collect and process the following information:

  • Personal & Account Data: When you create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your Name (name), Email Address (email and contactEmail), Gender (gender – Male/Female/Other), Profile Picture URL (profileImageUrl), and a unique User ID (uid). We also store basic account metadata such as role (normal_user), accountStatus (active), createdAt, and updatedAt timestamps.
  • Zero-Knowledge Secret Date (PIN): To protect your app lock screen (LockScreenActivity), you select a Secret Date (PIN). We never store your raw date in plaintext on our servers. Instead, it is hashed locally on your device using a 16-byte random cryptographic salt (secretDateSalt) and 65,536 iterations of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 into an irreversible one-way verifier (secretDateVerifier).
  • App Activity, Presence & Connection Data:
    • Hybrid Presence: We process your real-time online status (isOnline / online) and last active timestamp (lastSeen) across Cloud Firestore and Firebase Realtime Database.
    • Connection Requests: We store the status of connection requests you send or receive (requestId, senderId, receiverId, status – pending/approved/rejected, and timestamp).
    • Local Offline Cache: Approved friends and authentication states are cached locally on your device (LocalChatDbHelper SQLite database and encrypted SharedPreferences) so the app works smoothly even with weak or no internet.
  • Voice Call Signaling & Call Logs:
    • Temporary Call Signaling: When you make or receive an audio call, temporary signaling data (callerUid, receiverUid, call status, WebRTC offerSdp/answerSdp, and network ICE Candidates) is processed to establish the connection. This temporary signaling document and its sub-collections are automatically deleted from our servers immediately after the call ends.
    • Call History (call_logs): We store basic call logs including callId, caller and receiver names/UIDs, finalStatus, call duration (durationSeconds), and timestamp.
    • Live Voice Stream: Audio calls are transmitted directly via peer-to-peer WebRTC streams (LOVELINE_AUDIO_TRACK_01). We never record, listen to, or store your voice conversations on our servers.
  • Device Info & Crash Logs: To maintain network stability, route peer-to-peer calls, and fix software bugs, we collect device OS version, network state, IP addresses (used strictly for WebRTC STUN/ICE routing), and crash logs.

3Purpose of Data Collection

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing Core Services: Enabling user search, connection requests, real-time messaging, and low-bandwidth Opus-optimized peer-to-peer voice calling.
  • Account Security & Brute-Force Protection: Verifying your identity on the lock screen and enforcing a temporary 30-second lockout after 5 failed PIN attempts.
  • Busy Call Detection: Checking whether a recipient is already on another active call (in-app or cellular GSM call) so the caller can be notified with an accurate “Busy” status.
  • App Integrity & Customer Support: Detecting app tampering, fixing crashes, and responding to user support inquiries.

4Device Permissions We Request

In accordance with our AndroidManifest.xml, the Help app requests only the permissions necessary for its core features:

RECORD_AUDIO MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Required to capture and transmit your voice during an active audio call and manage audio routing (Earpiece/Speaker). You can mute your microphone at any time during a call.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC

Keeps your ongoing voice call active and displays a call status notification when the app is minimized in the background.

BLUETOOTH BLUETOOTH_ADMIN BLUETOOTH_CONNECT

Allows seamless routing of voice call audio to connected Bluetooth headsets or earphones.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT VIBRATE WAKE_LOCK

Used to wake the screen, vibrate, and display incoming call alerts even when your phone is locked.

INTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE ACCESS_WIFI_STATE CHANGE_WIFI_STATE

Monitors internet connectivity and acquires a low-latency WifiLock during active voice calls to prevent audio drops.

6Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, email address, Secret Date (PIN), or call logs to any third parties or advertisers.
  • With Other Users: Our User model strictly filters out sensitive fields (@Exclude and @IgnoreExtraProperties), ensuring your email address and Secret Date verifier are never loaded into another user’s device memory or UI. Other users can only view your Name, Profile Picture, Online status, and Last Seen status.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information only if required to do so by law, court order, or valid legal process from law enforcement authorities.

7Data Security

We implement multi-layered security controls to protect your information:

  • Encrypted Transit: All communication with Firebase servers occurs over HTTPS/SSL, and WebRTC voice streams are encrypted using DTLS/SRTP protocols.
  • Android Hardware Keystore (TEE): Offline PIN verification tokens are encrypted using the Android Hardware Keystore (AES-256-GCM NoPadding under alias LovelineHardwareVaultKey_v1), ensuring keys cannot be extracted even on rooted devices.
  • Screen Capture & Overlay Protection: Sensitive screens (LoginActivity, LockScreenActivity, IncomingCallActivity, and OngoingCallActivity) enforce FLAG_SECURE to prevent screenshots and screen recording. Action buttons enforce filterTouchesWhenObscured to prevent malicious tapjacking/overlay attacks.
  • Anti-Tamper & Hooking Checks: The app verifies its official SHA-1/SHA-256 signing certificate and checks for unauthorized hooking frameworks (Frida, Xposed, Substrate) or rooting to protect user sessions.

8Children’s Privacy

The Help app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has registered an account, we take immediate steps to delete the account and remove all associated data from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

9User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store requirements, you have full control over your personal data and can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records (Users profile, ChatRequests, local cache, and call_logs) at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the Help app, navigate to Profile / Settings, and tap “Delete Account” to permanently delete your account and associated data.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, you can request account and data deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request - Help App”. We will permanently erase all your data within 7 business days.
