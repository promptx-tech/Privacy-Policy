01

Introduction

Welcome to Bluetooth Audio Receiver (com.bluetoothreceiver.dev), developed and maintained by PromptX Tech. We deeply respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains in simple, transparent terms how our application functions, what permissions it requests, and how your information is handled.

02

Data Collection (Strict Zero Data Policy)

We DO NOT collect, record, store, save, or upload any personal or audio data to any external server.
  • Personal Information: Our app does not feature user registration, login, or sign-up screens. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or profile photo.
  • Audio Data: All audio captured from your Bluetooth headset microphone is processed strictly in real-time within your device's temporary memory buffer and is never recorded or saved as a file.
  • Device Info & Usage Logs: We do not collect or track your IP address, hardware identifiers, or usage analytics on any remote servers.
  • Local Preferences: The app only saves a local boolean flag (isPrivacyAccepted) inside your device's standard SharedPreferences (app_prefs) solely to remember that you have accepted this Privacy Policy. This data never leaves your phone.
03

Purpose of Audio Processing

All audio capturing and playback (via Android's AudioRecord and AudioTrack APIs) as well as real-time studio-grade audio enhancements (AcousticEchoCanceler, NoiseSuppressor, AutomaticGainControl, and DynamicsProcessing) happen 100% locally on your device.

The sole purpose of this processing is to capture live voice input from your connected wireless Bluetooth earphone/headset microphone and route it directly to your smartphone's built-in speaker in real-time.

04

Device Permissions We Request

To deliver seamless live audio routing, our app requests only the essential system permissions listed below:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required solely to capture live audio from your connected Bluetooth headset microphone and stream it immediately to your phone's speaker. No audio is ever stored.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used to check if a Bluetooth headset/earphone is actively connected and to establish the Bluetooth SCO communication link.
  • Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Required to switch audio modes, force speakerphone output, and toggle real-time Smart Audio effects.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Used to display an ongoing status notification ("Bluetooth Mic Active | Live Speaker Output") whenever live audio routing is running, ensuring full user awareness.
05

Third-Party Services

Bluetooth Audio Receiver does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob), cloud databases (such as Firebase), or analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics). The app is built using standard offline AndroidX libraries.

If you download or update the app through Google Play Store or another official app store, that platform's independent privacy policy governs your download experience:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
06

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or audio streams, we have no user data to sell, trade, rent, or share with advertisers or third parties.

07

Data Security

Your security is built directly into the offline architecture of our app. Because all microphone audio streams are routed locally inside your device hardware without internet transmission, there is zero risk of external interception. As soon as you press "Stop Listening" or close the app, the temporary audio buffer and hardware resources are immediately released and cleared.

08

Children's Privacy

Our application is a general-purpose audio utility tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

09

User Rights & Data Deletion

  • No Account Required: Because the app does not have user accounts or cloud storage, there is no server-side profile or personal data to delete.
  • Clearing Local Data & Permissions: You retain complete control over the app. You can revoke permissions or erase the local preference flag at any time by navigating to your phone's Settings > Apps > Bluetooth Receiver > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we introduce new features in future versions that alter how permissions or data are handled, we will update this page and revise the "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

प्रोसेसिंग का उद्देश्य (Purpose of Processing)

ऐप के अंदर होने वाली तमाम ऑडियो प्रोसेसिंग (जैसे AudioRecord और AudioTrack के ज़रिए आवाज़ को पकड़ना और सुनाना, तथा AcousticEchoCanceler, NoiseSuppressor और DynamicsProcessing के ज़रिए आवाज़ को साफ करना) पूरी तरह से 100% रियल-टाइम में आपके फोन के अंदर ही (Locally) होती है।

इसका एकमात्र उद्देश्य आपके कनेक्टेड ब्लूटूथ माइक की लाइव आवाज़ को बिना किसी रुकावट के तुरंत आपके फोन के स्पीकर तक पहुँचाना है।

डिवाइस की परमिशन (Device Permissions)

ऐप के मुख्य फीचर्स को चलाने के लिए हम केवल नीचे दी गई ज़रूरी परमिशन मांगते हैं:

  • माइक्रोफोन (RECORD_AUDIO): यह परमिशन केवल आपके कनेक्टेड ब्लूटूथ ईयरफोन के माइक से लाइव आवाज़ लेने और उसे रियल-टाइम में फोन के स्पीकर पर रूट (Route) करने के लिए ली जाती है। कोई भी आवाज़ सेव नहीं की जाती।
  • ब्लूटूथ (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): इसका उपयोग केवल यह जांचने के लिए होता है कि आपका वायरलेस ब्लूटूथ हेडसेट फोन से कनेक्ट है या नहीं, और उसके साथ ऑडियो लिंक (SCO) जोड़ने के लिए।
  • ऑडियो सेटिंग्स (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): ऑडियो रूटिंग को कंट्रोल करने, स्पीकरफोन मोड चालू करने और साउंड इफेक्ट्स (Smart Audio) को काम में लेने के लिए।
  • नोटिफिकेशन (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) और फोरग्राउंड सर्विस (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): जब लाइव ऑडियो रूटिंग चालू होती है, तो यूज़र की जानकारी के लिए नोटिफिकेशन पैनल में "Bluetooth Mic Active | Live Speaker Output" स्टेटस दिखाया जाता है, ताकि आपको हमेशा पता रहे कि माइक सक्रिय है।
